Biography

Ashton Danyel Simmonds (born April 5, 1995), known professionally as Daniel Caesar, is a Canadian R&B singer-songwriter from Oshawa, Ontario. Known for his soulful voice and intimate songwriting, he rose to fame with his 2017 debut album Freudian, featuring the hit singles "Get You" and "Best Part" (with H.E.R.), which won a Grammy for Best R&B Performance.

His music blends gospel influences with modern R&B, drawing comparisons to artists like D'Angelo and Frank Ocean. Following his breakthrough, he released CASE STUDY 01 (2019) and NEVER ENOUGH (2023), further establishing his signature sound.

Caesar has collaborated with major artists like Justin Bieber, Brandy, and John Mayer. Despite some controversies, he remains a defining voice in contemporary R&B.
